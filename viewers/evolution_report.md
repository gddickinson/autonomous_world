## Deep Analysis

### What IS Working (Dynamic Systems)

1. **NPC Movement**: ~25-55 NPCs move per 100-tick interval. NPCs walk between buildings, commute to workplaces, and patrol. Screenshots confirm visible position changes across the city of Thornwall.

2. **NPC Social Dynamics**: The event log shows active courting (Vex & Isolde, Delphine & Zariel, etc.), teaching (Corin taught Ximena about religion, Finn taught Daven about performance), and social interactions.

3. **Career/Promotion System**: NPCs get promoted (commoner to guard, commoner to clergy, Cook to Innkeeper, Woodcutter to Carpenter, Laborer to Porter). Job distribution changes over time.

4. **Kingdom Treasury Drain**: Kingdoms with armies (Ironthrone Fortress, Northwatch Keep) steadily lose treasury from army upkeep. Ironthrone lost ~411 gold over 4 days, Northwatch lost ~218.

5. **Health/Disease System**: Multiple NPCs contracted Food Poisoning from "spoiled winter stores" -- a seasonal health event.

6. **Monster Society**: A gnoll pack attempted a raid on Goldpeak and lost warriors.

7. **Creature Ecology**: Creature population dropped from 6487 to 4733 (dormancy culling mostly), with ongoing small losses (bat, deer, fox, minotaur deaths from needs/combat).

8. **NPC Work Activities**: Guards are guarding (32-35 at any time), farmers are farming, workers are collecting/building. The action distribution shows real work happening.

9. **Divine System**: Morwen petitioned about "Death has been cheated" -- the pantheon is generating narrative events.

### What is NOT Working (Static Systems)

1. **Terrain Tiles**: Zero tile changes in the 100x100 sample area. Farmland stays farmland, forest stays forest. The vegetation system tracks 0 tiles. Farmers are "farming" but not changing tiles. Woodcutters are not converting forest to stumps.

2. **Building Construction/Destruction**: Zero building count changes across all 29 settlements. The construction system exists but never fires -- possibly missing triggers (no kingdoms commissioning buildings, no NPC-driven build requests).

3. **Trade Caravans**: Zero active caravans at any point. The trade system is initialized but never dispatches caravans. Settlements have no inter-settlement goods flow.

4. **NPC Births/Deaths**: No NPC died and no NPC was born in 4 game days. The dormancy system prevents off-screen death (needs clamped at 15). Population is completely frozen.

5. **NPC Gold Economy**: Individual NPC gold never changed. No wages paid, no purchases made, no economic transactions between NPCs.

6. **Ground Items**: Zero ground items throughout. No drops, no litter, no trade goods left anywhere.

7. **World Events**: The event timer (random 30-90 seconds) should trigger droughts, storms, plagues etc. but zero world events occurred. Possibly the timer isn't advancing at the accelerated time speed.

### Key Structural Issues

1. **Dormancy System Dominates**: 118 NPCs (18%) were culled in the first 100 ticks as they moved beyond the active radius from the player. This is the largest single change and it's not "world evolution" -- it's just entity management.

2. **Time Speed vs System Thresholds**: Many systems use wall-clock timers (e.g., event_timer uses `time.time()`), not game-time. At 60x speed, game days pass but wall-clock-gated systems don't fire proportionally.

3. **Most NPCs are Idle**: 360-380 of ~526 alive NPCs are "idle" at any given tick (~70%). The decision/schedule system isn't giving them enough to do.

4. **No Economic Circulation**: Kingdoms lose treasury to upkeep but gold doesn't flow back from taxation, trade, or production. The economy is a one-way drain.

## Recommendations

### High Priority (World Feels Dead Without These)

- **Wire farming/woodcutting to tile changes**: When farmers "farm", convert adjacent grass to farmland/wheat. When woodcutters "work", convert forest to stumps. This is the most visible sign of a living world.

- **Enable NPC death/birth cycle**: Remove or relax the dormancy need clamping (needs pinned at 15 prevents all off-screen death). Add birth events from courting couples after pregnancy duration. Without population dynamics, the world is frozen.

- **Trigger trade caravans**: The TradeSystem exists but needs regular dispatch triggers. Merchants should load goods and travel between settlements.

- **Make construction actually build**: The ConstructionSystem and auto_commission exist but don't produce buildings. Kingdoms should commission buildings when treasury allows, and builders should complete them over time, adding to settlement.buildings.

### Medium Priority (Deepens Immersion)

- **Fix world event timer**: Use game-time not wall-clock for event scheduling so droughts, storms, and festivals actually occur at accelerated speeds.

- **NPC economic transactions**: Pay wages for work done, charge for food/drink at inns, enable merchant buying/selling. Individual gold should fluctuate.

- **Reduce idle NPC percentage**: 70% idle is too high. More NPCs need scheduled activities, patrol routes, or autonomous goals.

- **Terrain changes from conflict**: When gnoll raids happen or battles occur near settlements, damage nearby tiles (farmland to tilled soil, buildings destroyed).

### Lower Priority (Polish)

- **Vegetation system activation**: The grazing/recovery system exists but tracks 0 tiles. Creatures should trigger grazing when they eat, which degrades tiles.

- **Road construction**: New roads between growing settlements would show infrastructure evolution.

- **Mine establishment/depletion**: Resource extraction sites that appear and eventually run dry.

- **Seasonal crop cycles**: Wheat fields should grow in spring/summer and be harvested in autumn, visibly changing tile appearance.
